Blobunnies

Nickname: Bunn

Reference Art:




Blobunny (Lepus Spheroformis, also known as "Bunn") is a fascinating species commonly found in Deltadim. This creature resembles a fluffy ball, allowing to roll and bounce with surprising agility.

The Blobunny thrives in colonies ranging from 5 to 20 individuals. With their petite size, measuring a mere 5 inches, these squishy creatures are surely huggable and can provide comfort as a pet :3
